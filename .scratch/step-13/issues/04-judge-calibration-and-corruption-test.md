# 04: Judge calibration (B) and the corruption test (C)

**What to build:** The user scores 30 dev answers through `python -m eval.calibrate`, with each answer's claims split beforehand by a separate claude-sonnet-5-5 call, and a report computes agreement with the judge per score type. `python -m eval.corrupt` plants four kinds of known error in 30 other dev answers and reports how often the judge catches each. `results.md` labels judged columns by the calibration result and shows the catch rates. - [ ] Draw: 30 dev answers by fixed seed from the committed dev run files, stratified by arm and class; `benchmarks/calibration/draw.json`, write-once.
- [ ] `python -m eval.calibration.claims` (network): `prompts/claims/v1.md` pinned, claude-sonnet-5-5 at effort `high`, structured `{claims: [{sentence_index, text}]}`, through the response cache, estimate first (about $0.10); `benchmarks/calibration/claims.jsonl`.
- [ ] `eval.calibrate --reviewer "<name>"`: refuses non-interactive input; shows question, kept sentences, retrieved chunks with IDs and claims; per claim supported / not supported / not a claim, with a reason for every "not supported", and missed claims addable; relevancy 1–5 on the written rubric; citation support yes or no per kept sentence; decline and not-found correctness for those records. Judge scores hidden until the answer is saved. Time per answer logged; under 15 s prompts "take another look?". Appends to `benchmarks/calibration/scores-{reviewer}.jsonl`; resumes at the first unscored answer. `--rescore 10` hides earlier entries.
- [ ] `python -m eval.calibration.report` (offline): faithfulness and relevancy (rubric mapped by (r−1)/4) against the judge's 3-run mean by Spearman's rho with Fisher-z interval, MAE and signed bias; citation support per kept sentence by Cohen's kappa against the judge's 3-run majority verdict (from cached judge replies), bootstrap by answer; decline and not-found by percent agreement and disagreement count; judge self-agreement across runs; re-score agreement when present. Pass at a point estimate of 0.6 or more. `benchmarks/calibration/report.json`.
- [ ] `eval.corrupt`: four deterministic corruptions per answer (changed number, swapped fiscal period, wrong-chunk citation, added unsupported sentence), skips counted; 30 dev answers by its own seed, disjoint from the calibration draw; judged for faithfulness and citation support, 3 runs, estimate first (about $0.60); `benchmarks/calibration/corruption-run{N}.json` with catch rates and Wilson intervals, labelled "corruption test, not calibration".
- [ ] `results.md`: passing judged columns labelled "calibrated, n = 30, claims split by an agent"; failing columns leave the headline table; calibration statistics and catch rates in the measured-numbers block.
- [ ] Tests, seen failing first: `eval.calibrate` through the review fakes (non-interactive refusal, hidden scores, resume, pace prompt, required reason, re-score hiding); the draw; statistics on small known cases; corruptors on real dev answers (one change each, deterministic, skips); catch rates from recorded judge replies; `results.md` labels for pass and fail.
