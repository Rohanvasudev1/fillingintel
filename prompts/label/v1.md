## The rule

Two nodes are the same entity when they refer to one real-world thing at the same level. Examples:

- "Groq" and "Groq, Inc." are the same entity: one company, written with and without its legal suffix.
- A parent and a subsidiary are the same entity only where the filing uses the two names interchangeably for one business.
- A product family and its member are different entities: "Blackwell" and "GB200" are not the same.
- A product and a service built on it are different entities, as are a regulation and an amendment or a regional version of it ("GDPR" and "U.K. GDPR").
- An acronym and its full name are the same entity when the evidence shows they name one thing ("SEC" and "Securities and Exchange Commission").
- Two metric values are the same entity when they are one reported figure: the same subject, period, value and unit, under two names ("Revenue" and "Total revenue").
